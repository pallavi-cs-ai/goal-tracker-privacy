Goal Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Goal Tracker is a Chrome extension designed to help users create goals, track progress, set deadlines and priorities, and review completed goals.

Goal Data

Goal Tracker stores your goal information locally in your browser using Chrome's storage functionality. This includes information such as goal names, targets, progress, deadlines, priorities, and completed goals.

Goal Tracker does not transmit your goal data to the developer or to an external server.

Feedback

Goal Tracker includes an optional feedback form. If you choose to submit feedback, the information you provide may include your rating, feedback type, message, usage frequency, and an optional email address.

Feedback submissions are transmitted to Formspree, a third-party form processing service, for the purpose of receiving and managing user feedback.

Submitting feedback is voluntary. Goal Tracker does not require an email address to use the extension.

Data Sharing

Goal Tracker does not sell user data. Information submitted through the feedback form is used only to receive feedback and improve the extension. It is not used for advertising, lending, or determining creditworthiness.

Browsing Activity

Goal Tracker does not collect or monitor your browsing history, the websites you visit, or the content of webpages you view.

Accounts

Goal Tracker does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Goal Tracker's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
